Motor Insurance - Flood Damage Guide

Section 1: Flood Damage
Some comprehensive motor insurance policies may provide cover for accidental vehicle damage caused by flooding or water ingress.

Section 2: Coverage Conditions
Flood coverage is subject to the terms of the customer's specific policy, policy status, exclusions, excess, and any applicable endorsements.

Section 3: Evidence
Customers may be asked to provide:
- Photographs of the damaged vehicle
- Incident location and date
- Vehicle registration information
- Repair estimate
- Claim form
- Other supporting evidence requested by the insurer

Section 4: Important Notice
This guide provides general information only. It does not confirm that a particular customer's policy covers flood damage.
