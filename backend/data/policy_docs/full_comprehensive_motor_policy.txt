# FULL COMPREHENSIVE MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY
Document Type: Policy Manual
Policy Category: Full Comprehensive (full_comprehensive)
Scope: Synthetic university demonstration policy terms and coverage guidelines.

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND SCOPE OF COVERAGE
This Full Comprehensive Motor Insurance Policy provides total coverage for the insured vehicle against accidental loss or damage, as well as comprehensive liability to third parties. Under this full comprehensive policy category, the policyholder is protected for both own-vehicle damage and legal liabilities arising from vehicle use.

SECTION 2: OWN-VEHICLE COLLISION DAMAGE COVERAGE
The policy provides full accidental damage cover for the insured vehicle resulting from collisions, overturning, impacts with stationary objects, or road traffic accidents.
- Covers accidental collision damage to all vehicle panels including front bumper, rear bumper, bonnet, boot, doors, and roof.
- Covers structural and body repairs, certified replacement parts, and towing fees to the nearest approved repair facility.
- Applicable standard excess: £250 per accidental collision claim.

SECTION 3: FIRE, LIGHTNING, AND EXPLOSION COVERAGE
The policy covers loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused directly by accidental fire, external explosion, self-ignition, lightning, or electrical fire.
- Full repair or market value replacement if total loss occurs due to fire.
- No excess is charged for non-fault accidental fire claims where the vehicle was stationary and secured.

SECTION 4: THEFT AND BREAK-IN COVERAGE
The policy covers total vehicle theft, attempted vehicle theft, and malicious break-ins.
- Covers physical damage caused to door locks, ignition barrels, and window glass during attempted break-in.
- Covers replacement of stolen vehicle if not recovered within 28 days of police crime reporting.
- Standard theft deductible: £250.

SECTION 5: FLOOD, STORM, AND NATURAL DISASTER COVERAGE
This full comprehensive policy covers loss or damage caused by storm, hail, tempest, flood water, and rising surface water.
- Covers engine hydro-lock repairs and interior water extraction when caused by unexpected flood water.
- Covers vehicle body repairs following storm debris impacts or falling tree branches.

SECTION 6: WINDSCREEN AND WINDOW GLASS REPLACEMENT
Full coverage is provided for accidental breakage or cracking of the vehicle windscreen, side windows, rear screen, and fixed panoramic glass roofs.
- Windscreen repair (stone chips up to 10mm): £0 excess.
- Complete windscreen replacement using approved glass providers: £75 excess.
- Claiming for glass damage under this section does not affect your no-claims bonus.

SECTION 7: MALICIOUS DAMAGE AND VANDALISM
Covers intentional damage, scratching, paint stripping, tyre slashing, and vandalism caused by unknown malicious third parties, subject to a valid police report reference.

SECTION 8: THIRD-PARTY LIABILITY COVERAGE
Provides unlimited legal indemnity for third-party bodily injury or death, and up to £20,000,000 indemnity for third-party property damage caused by the insured vehicle.

SECTION 9: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
Coverage under this full comprehensive policy is strictly excluded if:
- The driver was operating the vehicle without a valid driving licence or under the influence of alcohol or unprescribed drugs.
- The vehicle was being used for motor racing, pace-making, or speed trials.
- The damage was caused deliberately by the insured policyholder or family member.
