Motor Insurance Comprehensive Guide

What is Motor Insurance

Motor insurance is a contract between you and the insurance company that protects you financially in case your vehicle is involved in an accident, theft, fire, natural disaster, or other covered events. In Sri Lanka, third party motor insurance is mandatory by law for all vehicles on public roads.

Types of Motor Insurance Coverage

Third Party Only Coverage
This is the minimum legal requirement. It covers damage or injury caused to other people, their vehicles, or their property by your vehicle. It does not cover damage to your own vehicle. This is the most affordable option.

Third Party Fire and Theft Coverage
This includes everything in Third Party Only coverage plus protection if your vehicle is stolen or damaged by fire. It does not cover accidental damage to your own vehicle.

Comprehensive Coverage
This is the most complete coverage available. It covers damage to your own vehicle from accidents, third party damage and injuries, fire damage, theft and break-in, natural disasters, flood, vandalism, and windscreen damage. Comprehensive coverage gives you the best financial protection.

What Comprehensive Motor Insurance Covers

Accident Damage
If your vehicle is damaged in a road accident, comprehensive insurance covers the cost of repairs. You will need to pay your excess or deductible first. The remaining repair costs are covered by the insurance company.

Theft Coverage
If your vehicle is stolen, the insurance company will pay you the market value or agreed value of the vehicle minus your excess. You must report the theft to the police within 24 hours and provide the police report to the insurer.

Fire Damage
If your vehicle is damaged or destroyed by fire whether accidental or caused by another party, the insurance company will cover repair or replacement costs.

Flood and Water Damage
If your vehicle is damaged by flood or rising water, comprehensive insurance covers the repair costs. You must report this promptly and document the damage with photographs.

Windscreen Damage
Many comprehensive policies include windscreen replacement or repair. Some policies allow one free windscreen claim per year without affecting your no claims bonus.

Third Party Liability
If you cause an accident that injures another person or damages their property, your insurance covers the legal costs and compensation payments to the other party.

What is Not Covered - Common Exclusions

Driving Without a Valid Licence
If you were driving without a valid driving licence at the time of the accident, your claim will be rejected.

Driving Under the Influence
If you were under the influence of alcohol or drugs at the time of the accident, your claim will be rejected and you may face criminal charges.

Intentional Damage
Any damage caused intentionally is not covered.

Mechanical Breakdown
Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, and maintenance issues are not covered by motor insurance.

Using Vehicle for Hire Without Correct Policy
If you use a private vehicle for hire or commercial purposes without a commercial policy, your claim may be rejected.

Unapproved Modifications
Modifications to your vehicle that were not declared to and approved by the insurer may void your coverage.

How to Make a Motor Insurance Claim

Report the Accident Immediately
Notify your insurance company within 24 hours of the accident. Late reporting can affect your claim.

File a Police Report
Obtain a police report from the nearest police station. This is mandatory for all accident claims.

Document the Damage
Take clear photographs of all damage to your vehicle and any other vehicles involved.

Submit Your Claim
Complete the claim form and submit it along with your police report, photographs, driving licence, vehicle registration, and insurance certificate.

Vehicle Assessment
The insurance company will send an assessor to inspect the damage and estimate repair costs.

Claim Settlement
Once approved, the insurance company will either authorize repairs at an approved garage or pay you the assessed value.

No Claims Bonus

The No Claims Bonus is a discount on your premium for every year you do not make a claim. After one claim-free year you receive a discount. After five consecutive claim-free years you can receive up to 50 percent discount on your premium. Making a claim will reduce or remove your no claims bonus.

Premium Factors

Your motor insurance premium is calculated based on the vehicle make and model, vehicle age and value, engine capacity, your age and driving experience, your claims history, the type of coverage selected, and the geographic area where the vehicle is primarily used.

Policy Renewal

Motor insurance policies typically run for one year. You will receive a renewal notice before your policy expires. It is important to renew before the expiry date to maintain continuous coverage. Driving with an expired policy is illegal.
