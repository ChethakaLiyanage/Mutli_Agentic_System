Motor Insurance - Claim Reporting Guidance

Section 1: Prompt Reporting
Customers should report incidents as soon as reasonably possible after they occur.

Section 2: Delayed Reporting
Significant reporting delays may require additional review.

Section 3: Supporting Information
When reporting a claim, customers should provide the incident date, location, description, and available supporting documents.

Section 4: Policy Terms
Exact reporting requirements and deadlines depend on the customer's individual policy wording.
