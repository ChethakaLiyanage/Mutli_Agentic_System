==================================================
VEHICLE FLOOD INCIDENTS
==================================================

A vehicle flood incident occurs when water enters or damages a vehicle
because of flooding, heavy rainfall, overflowing waterways, or similar
water-related events.

Customers should avoid attempting to start a vehicle when significant
water has entered the engine compartment.

==================================================
FLOOD CLAIM REPORTING PERIOD
==================================================

For this sample procedure, a vehicle flood incident must be reported
within 13 days from the date of the incident.

The 13-day period is a synthetic test rule used only for this university
project.

==================================================
VEHICLE AREAS AFFECTED BY FLOODING
==================================================

Flood water may cause damage to multiple areas of a vehicle.

Possible affected areas include:

- Engine
- Air intake system
- Transmission
- Battery
- Alternator
- Starter motor
- Electrical wiring
- Electronic control modules
- Sensors
- Fuse boxes
- Dashboard electronics
- Infotainment system
- Front seats
- Rear seats
- Carpets
- Interior trim
- Boot / trunk
- Underbody
- Braking components
- Wheel bearings
- Exhaust system

==================================================
FLOOD DAMAGE CLASSIFICATION
==================================================

Flood-related damage may include:

- Water ingress
- Engine water damage
- Electrical failure
- Electronic component damage
- Interior water damage
- Corrosion
- Contamination
- Mud and debris damage
- Underbody damage

==================================================
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR A FLOOD CLAIM
==================================================

For this sample flood claim procedure, the customer should provide:

1. Vehicle registration document
2. Valid driving licence
3. Photographs of the vehicle
4. Photographs showing the flood or water damage
5. Description of the flood incident
6. Location where the flooding occurred
7. One vehicle inspection report

Additional evidence may be requested during claim review when necessary.

==================================================
FLOOD VEHICLE INSPECTION
==================================================

For this sample procedure, a flood-damaged vehicle must receive an
inspection before major flood-related repairs are started.

The inspection should identify visible water damage and any affected
mechanical, electrical, electronic, and interior components.

==================================================
CUSTOMER GUIDANCE
==================================================

When reporting a flood incident, the customer should provide accurate
information about:

- Date of flooding
- Location
- Approximate water level where known
- Vehicle areas affected
- Whether the engine was running
- Whether an attempt was made to restart the vehicle
- Visible damage
