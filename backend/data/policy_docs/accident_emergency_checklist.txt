Accident Emergency Checklist and First Response

I Just Had an Accident - Immediate Steps

If you have just had a road accident, here is exactly what you need to do right now.

Are You Safe
First check if you are in immediate danger. If your vehicle is on fire or in a dangerous position, get out immediately and move away. Otherwise stay in your vehicle with your seatbelt on until it is safe.

Is Anyone Hurt
Check yourself. Check your passengers. Check the other driver and passengers if it is safe to approach. If anyone is hurt, do not wait and call the ambulance immediately on 1990. Do not try to move someone who is seriously injured unless there is fire or immediate danger of further injury.

Call the Ambulance First If Anyone Is Injured
Dial 1990 for ambulance. Tell them your location as precisely as possible. Tell them how many people are injured. Stay on the line and follow their instructions. If you are in a rural area or if 1990 does not connect, call 119 police who will dispatch medical help.

Call the Police
Dial 119 and report the accident. Give your name, the location of the accident, how many vehicles are involved, and whether anyone is injured. The police will come to the scene. Wait for them. Do not leave the scene of the accident as this is a criminal offence.

Turn On Your Hazard Lights
If you have not already done so, turn on your hazard warning lights. If you have a warning triangle in your vehicle, place it at least 50 meters behind your vehicle to warn other drivers.

Do Not Argue With the Other Driver
Stay calm and do not argue or get into a physical confrontation with the other driver. Simply exchange information politely. Arguments at the scene can escalate and will not help your insurance claim.

What to Tell the Agent When Filing a Claim

When you call us or submit your claim, please have ready:
- Your full name and policy number
- Date, time, and exact location of accident
- Your description of how the accident happened
- Number of vehicles involved
- Names and contact details of other drivers
- Vehicle registration numbers of all vehicles
- Police report number
- Details of any injuries
- Whether you have taken photographs

Emotional Support After an Accident

Being involved in a road accident is a stressful and traumatic experience. It is normal to feel shaken, anxious, or upset after an accident. Take a few deep breaths. If you are too shaken to make calls, ask a bystander to help. Your safety and health come first. Everything else including the insurance claim can be handled once you are safe.

Do Not Post on Social Media
Do not post about the accident on social media before you have spoken to your insurance company. Posts describing the accident or admitting any form of responsibility can affect your claim.

Quick Reference Emergency Numbers

Ambulance: 1990
Police: 119
Fire Brigade: 110
Suwa Seriya Ambulance: 1990
Insurance Claims Hotline: Contact your insurance company immediately after calling emergency services

Key Rules to Remember

Always call emergency services before worrying about insurance paperwork. Your safety and the safety of others is the absolute first priority. The insurance claim can always be filed later but injuries need immediate attention. You have 24 hours to notify your insurance company but emergency services should be called in the first few minutes.
