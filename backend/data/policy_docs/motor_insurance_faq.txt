Motor Insurance Frequently Asked Questions

What is motor insurance
Motor insurance is a contract that protects you financially if your vehicle is involved in an accident, theft, fire, or other covered event. It also covers your legal liability if you cause injury or damage to another person or their property.

Is motor insurance mandatory in Sri Lanka
Yes. Third party motor insurance is mandatory by law for all vehicles on public roads in Sri Lanka. Driving without valid insurance is a criminal offence.

What types of motor insurance are available
There are three main types. Third Party Only covers damage you cause to others. Third Party Fire and Theft adds protection for fire and theft of your own vehicle. Comprehensive covers all of the above plus accidental damage to your own vehicle.

How much does motor insurance cost
The premium depends on your vehicle type, age, engine capacity, your driving history, claims history, and the type of coverage you choose. Comprehensive coverage costs more than third party only but gives you much better protection.

What is an excess or deductible
The excess is the amount you must pay out of your own pocket when making a claim. For example if your excess is 25000 rupees and the repair cost is 100000 rupees, you pay 25000 and the insurance company pays 75000 rupees.

What is no claims bonus
The no claims bonus is a discount on your premium for each year you do not make a claim. The more years without a claim the bigger the discount. Making a claim may reduce your no claims bonus.

How do I make a claim after an accident
Report the accident to your insurance company within 24 hours. File a police report. Take photographs of the damage. Complete and submit the claim form with all required documents. An assessor will inspect your vehicle. Once approved, repairs will be authorized or a settlement will be paid.

What documents do I need for a claim
You need your completed claim form, police report, photographs of damage, copy of driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance certificate, and repair estimates. For injury claims you also need medical reports.

How long does it take to process a claim
Most claims are processed within 14 to 30 days depending on the complexity. Simple repair claims are typically faster. Claims involving injuries, disputes, or total loss may take longer.

Can my claim be rejected
Yes. Common reasons for rejection include driving without a valid licence, driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, late reporting beyond 24 hours, failure to provide a police report, intentional damage, using the vehicle for commercial purposes without proper coverage, and undisclosed vehicle modifications.

What happens if my car is a total loss
If the repair cost exceeds a certain percentage of the vehicle value usually 75 percent, the vehicle is declared a total loss. The insurance company will pay you the market value or agreed value of the vehicle minus your excess.

Can I choose my own repair garage
Many policies require you to use approved garages. Using an approved garage simplifies the process as the insurer pays the garage directly. If you use a non-approved garage, you may need to pay upfront and then claim reimbursement.

What should I do if I am in an accident right now
If you are in an accident right now, first make sure you and everyone else is safe. If anyone is injured call the ambulance on 1990 immediately. Call the police on 119. Turn on your hazard lights. Do not move the vehicles unless they are blocking traffic dangerously. Take photographs. Exchange details with the other driver. Do not admit fault. Then call your insurance company within 24 hours.

Does my insurance cover flood damage
Comprehensive motor insurance typically covers flood and water damage to your vehicle. You need to report it promptly and document the damage with photographs.

Does my insurance cover if someone else drives my car
It depends on your policy. Some policies cover any licensed driver. Others cover only named drivers. Check your policy document or contact your insurer to confirm.

What happens if the other driver is at fault
If the other driver caused the accident, their insurance should cover your repairs. However you may still need to file a claim with your own insurer who will recover costs from the other party insurer through subrogation.

What if I hit a pothole and damage my vehicle
Pothole damage to your vehicle is generally covered under comprehensive motor insurance. Document the pothole and the damage with photographs and report it to your insurer.

Am I covered if my car is vandalized
Yes, vandalism is covered under comprehensive motor insurance. Report the vandalism to the police first, then file a claim with your insurer.

What happens to my premium after a claim
Making a claim may increase your premium at renewal and may reduce your no claims bonus. The exact impact depends on the nature and cost of the claim.
