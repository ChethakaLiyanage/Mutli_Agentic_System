Motor Insurance - Windscreen Claims

Section 1: Windscreen Damage
Some motor insurance policies provide cover for accidental windscreen or window-glass damage.

Section 2: Common Evidence
The customer may be asked to provide:
- Photograph of the damaged windscreen
- Vehicle registration
- Policy details
- Incident date
- Description of how the damage occurred
- Repair or replacement estimate

Section 3: Policy Limits
Windscreen cover may be subject to limits, excess amounts, approved repair providers, or other policy-specific conditions.

Section 4: Specific Policy Confirmation
General windscreen guidance does not confirm cover under an individual customer's policy.
