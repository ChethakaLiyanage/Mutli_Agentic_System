MOTOR COLLISION CLAIM REPORTING
==================================================

A motor vehicle collision must be reported within 21 days from the date
of the accident.

The customer should provide the accident date, accident location,
vehicle details, and a description of the damage when reporting the claim.

==================================================
VEHICLE DAMAGE
==================================================

The following vehicle damage areas may be recorded during a collision claim:

- Front bumper
- Rear bumper
- Bonnet
- Boot
- Front windscreen
- Rear windscreen
- Side windows
- Left front door
- Left rear door
- Right front door
- Right rear door
- Left side mirror
- Right side mirror
- Front headlights
- Rear lights
- Front fenders
- Rear quarter panels
- Roof
- Wheels
- Tyres
- Engine area
- Underbody

Damage may include:

- Cracked
- Broken
- Shattered
- Scratched
- Dented
- Bent
- Crushed
- Burned
- Flooded
- Detached

==================================================
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR COLLISION CLAIM
==================================================

For this sample policy, a collision claim requires:

1. Vehicle registration document
2. Valid driving licence
3. Photographs of the damaged vehicle
4. Accident description
5. One repair estimate

A police report should also be provided when required by the circumstances
of the accident.

==================================================
WINDSCREEN DAMAGE
==================================================

Windscreen damage includes cracking, breaking, or shattering of the
front windscreen.

For this sample procedure, the customer should provide photographs of
the damaged windscreen and vehicle information when submitting the claim.

==================================================
FIRE DAMAGE
==================================================

Fire-related vehicle damage should be reported with photographs of the
damaged vehicle and a description of how the incident occurred.

==================================================
FLOOD DAMAGE
==================================================

Flood damage may include water entering the engine, electrical system,
interior, or other vehicle components.

The customer should provide photographs and details about the location
and circumstances of the flood incident.
