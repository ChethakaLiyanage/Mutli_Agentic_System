Immediate Accident Response Guide

What To Do Right After a Road Accident

If you have just been involved in a road accident, follow these steps immediately.

Step 1 - Ensure Your Safety First
Stay calm. Do not panic. Turn on your hazard lights immediately. If the vehicle is safe to stay in and you are not injured, remain inside until it is safe to exit. If there is smoke, fire, or fuel leakage, exit the vehicle immediately and move away from the car.

Step 2 - Check for Injuries
Check yourself for injuries. Check your passengers for injuries. Do not move anyone who appears to have a neck or spinal injury unless there is immediate danger such as fire. Ask anyone nearby if they are hurt.

Step 3 - Call Emergency Services Immediately
If anyone is injured, call an ambulance right away. Dial 1990 for ambulance in Sri Lanka or 119 for police. Do not delay calling for help even if the injuries seem minor. Emergency services must be notified as soon as possible. If you are unsure whether someone is injured, always call an ambulance. It is better to be safe.

Step 4 - Call the Police
Call the police immediately after any accident regardless of how minor it seems. Dial 119 for Sri Lanka Police. The police will come to the scene, assess the situation, and file an official accident report. This police report is required when making an insurance claim. Without a police report, your claim may be rejected.

Step 5 - Do Not Move the Vehicles Unless Absolutely Necessary
Do not move the vehicles involved in the accident until the police arrive, unless the vehicles are blocking traffic and creating a safety hazard. If you must move vehicles, take photographs first.

Step 6 - Collect Evidence at the Scene
Take photographs of all vehicles involved showing damage from multiple angles. Photograph the accident scene including road markings, traffic signs, and road conditions. Photograph any injuries if safe to do so. Note the time, date, and exact location of the accident. Write down weather conditions and visibility.

Step 7 - Exchange Information with the Other Party
Get the full name of the other driver. Get their National Identity Card number or driving licence number. Get their vehicle registration number. Get their insurance company name and policy number. Get their phone number. Do not argue about fault at the scene.

Step 8 - Get Witness Details
If there are any witnesses, politely ask for their name and contact number. Witness statements can help support your insurance claim.

Step 9 - Do Not Admit Fault
Do not say sorry or admit fault at the scene even if you think you may be responsible. Fault determination is handled by the insurance company and police. Any admission at the scene can be used against your claim.

Step 10 - Contact Your Insurance Company
Call your insurance company as soon as possible after the accident. Our claims hotline is available. Notify us within 24 hours of the accident. Provide us with the police report number, accident details, and photos.

Emergency Numbers Sri Lanka
Police: 119
Ambulance: 1990
Fire: 110
Accident Service: 1938
