Motor Insurance - Theft Claim Requirements

Section 1: Immediate Actions
If a vehicle is stolen or broken into, the customer should report the incident to the police as soon as possible.

Section 2: Common Required Documents
A theft-related claim may require:
- Completed claim form
- Police report or police reference
- Vehicle registration document
- Policy information
- Proof of identity
- Photographs where relevant
- Information about keys
- Other documents requested by the insurer

Section 3: Review
The insurer may review the circumstances of the loss, policy status, supporting evidence, and claim history before a human claims officer makes a final decision.

Section 4: No Automatic Decision
Automated systems may support claim processing and risk triage, but the final claim decision is made by an authorized human claims officer.
