Motor Insurance - Common Exclusions Guide

Section 1: General Exclusions
Motor insurance policies may contain exclusions. Examples may include:
- Loss occurring outside the policy period
- Use of the vehicle outside permitted policy conditions
- Deliberate or intentional damage
- Unapproved commercial use where excluded
- Driving without required legal authorization
- Certain mechanical or electrical failures not caused by an insured event

Section 2: Policy-Specific Terms
Exclusions vary by policy. The customer's actual policy wording must be checked before determining whether an exclusion applies.

Section 3: No Automatic Rejection
The presence of a possible exclusion must not automatically result in claim rejection by an automated system. Final decisions require human review.
