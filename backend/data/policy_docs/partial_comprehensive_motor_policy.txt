# PARTIAL COMPREHENSIVE MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY
Document Type: Policy Manual
Policy Category: Partial Comprehensive (partial_comprehensive)
Scope: Synthetic university demonstration policy terms and coverage guidelines.

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND SCOPE OF COVERAGE
This Partial Comprehensive Motor Insurance Policy provides defined protection covering fire, theft, natural flood disaster, windscreen glass, and third-party liabilities. Under this partial comprehensive policy, ordinary accidental own-vehicle collision damage is explicitly excluded.

SECTION 2: OWN-VEHICLE COLLISION DAMAGE EXCLUSION
CRITICAL POLICY RESTRICTION: Ordinary own-vehicle accidental collision damage is strictly EXCLUDED under this Partial Comprehensive policy.
- Accidental impact damage to front bumper, rear bumper, side panels, doors, or wheels caused by collisions, overturning, or driver error is NOT covered.
- The policyholder is personally responsible for the cost of repairing their own vehicle following any road traffic collision or impact accident.
- To obtain collision damage protection for your vehicle, an upgrade to Full Comprehensive coverage is required.

SECTION 3: FIRE AND LIGHTNING COVERAGE
The policy provides full cover for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidental fire, self-ignition, explosion, or lightning strike.
- Covers fire damage repairs or market value total loss settlement.
- Standard fire excess of £200 applies.

SECTION 4: THEFT AND ATTEMPTED THEFT COVERAGE
The policy covers total loss of the vehicle resulting from theft, as well as damage caused during attempted break-in or theft.
- Covers repair of ignition barrels, broken locks, and stolen vehicle recovery costs.
- Requires immediate notification to the police and a formal crime reference number.
- Applicable theft deductible: £250.

SECTION 5: FLOOD AND NATURAL DISASTER WATER DAMAGE
The policy provides coverage for loss or damage caused directly by flood water, river overflow, severe weather, and stormwater inundation.
- Covers professional water removal, electrical drying, and flood repairs up to the vehicle market value limit.

SECTION 6: WINDSCREEN AND GLASS COVERAGE
Covers accidental damage to the front windscreen, side windows, and rear glass.
- Stone chip repair: £25 excess.
- Complete windscreen replacement: £100 excess.

SECTION 7: THIRD-PARTY LIABILITY COVERAGE
Provides statutory legal liability cover for injury or death to third parties, and up to £10,000,000 indemnity for damage caused to third-party vehicles or property.

SECTION 8: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
Coverage under this partial comprehensive policy does NOT apply to:
- Any own-vehicle collision or road accident impact damage (as specified in Section 2).
- Vandalism or malicious scratches not associated with an attempted theft.
- Wear, tear, mechanical breakdown, or electrical failure.
- Driving without a valid driving licence or under illegal influence.
