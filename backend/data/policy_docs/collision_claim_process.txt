Motor Insurance - Collision Claim Process

Section 1: Incident Reporting
Following a vehicle collision, the customer should provide:
- Date of incident
- Location
- Description of the collision
- Details of vehicle damage
- Other parties involved, where applicable

Section 2: Supporting Documents
Common supporting documents may include:
- Claim form
- Vehicle registration
- Driving licence where required
- Damage photographs
- Repair estimate
- Police report where required
- Third-party information where available

Section 3: Assessment
The claim may be checked against the active policy, claim history, submitted documents, and other claim information.

Section 4: Human Review
Automated systems may assist with information retrieval and risk triage. An authorized claims officer remains responsible for consequential claim decisions.
